# OUTDATED AND TIME-SENSITIVE INFORMATION

Claims in this knowledge base that **depend on a moment in time** and should not be treated as
durable knowledge.

> **Corpus capture date: September 2026.** Knowledge base built: **16 September 2026.**
>
> **Only 35 of 143 sources carry a determinable publication date.** For the other 108, currency
> cannot be assessed at all. This is the single largest limitation on the corpus's time-sensitive
> content.

---

## 1. The rule

> **Method ages slowly. Findings, tools, figures and trends age fast.**

When retrieving from this knowledge base, an agent should:

1. **State the date** of any specific claim it repeats.
2. **Prefer the durable layer.** "How to analyse trends" outlives "these are the trends."
3. **Refuse to present §2 and §3 content as current** without re-verification.
