## When

When feedback is wanted and "I don't like it" is not useful.

## Method

1. Tie every comment to a principle from `product-design-fundamentals.md`, or to an observed
   usability problem — route that to `specify-and-audit-a-usability-test`.
2. Where the failure spans touchpoints rather than screens, use service design **as a
   diagnostic lens**: the problem may be in the handoffs, not the interface.

## Output

Feedback, each item attached to a principle or an observation.

## What this cannot claim

Service design as a method. `service-design.md` states its own limitation — **"No method."**
— and its primary artifact, the service blueprint, *"is named but never explained anywhere in
the corpus."* It is a lens here and nothing more.
