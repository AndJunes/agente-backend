## When

When there is something to transform. It is the wrong tool for a blank page.

## Method

Apply the lenses — substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to another use, eliminate,
reverse. **How many to use is genuinely open**: one source presents a systematic sweep of all
seven, another says *"focus on one or two SCAMPER lenses per session rather than trying to
apply all seven."* The corpus calls this *"a real tension… both are defensible"* and leaves
it to session length and goal. Say which you chose.

## Output

Variants grouped by lens.

## What this cannot claim

Its own history. One source credits Eberle alone, another gives Osborn (1953) → Eberle; both
are UNVERIFIED and the corpus notes that the first alone would mislead.
