## When

Between a problem statement and any generation of options.

## Method

Apply the technique as the document describes it, radiating from the problem rather than from
a solution. The output is an input to `run-an-ideation-session`, not a substitute for it.

## Output

A map of the parts and how they relate.

## What this cannot claim

That a mapped problem is an understood one.
