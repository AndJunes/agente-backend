## When

After generation, never during it. Mixing the two suppresses the generation.

## Method

1. Agree the criteria before looking at the options.
2. Evaluate against them.
3. Keep the discarded options and why — the reasoning is what makes the shortlist defensible
   later, and hands `assess-a-product-opportunity` its alternatives.

## Output

A shortlist, and a record of what was set aside and why.

## What this cannot claim

A threshold. As everywhere in this corpus, there is no number that says "pursue this".
