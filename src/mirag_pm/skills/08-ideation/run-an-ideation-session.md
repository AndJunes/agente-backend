## When

After the problem is framed. The corpus carries an explicit skip condition —
*"haven't done basic research"* — and it is the most useful thing in the document.

## Method

1. **Check the skip conditions first.** A session held before research produces opinions.
2. If it proceeds, prefer a written-first format; see `run-a-6-3-5-brainwriting-round`.
3. Converge with `converge-on-a-shortlist`, which is a separate operation and should not
   happen in the same breath as generation.

Note the unresolved tension the corpus records: one source places ideation at the *end* of
discovery, another lists *"identify and frame problems worth solving"* as a reason to
brainstorm — i.e. early. The corpus does not reconcile these. Say which you are doing.

## Output

A session design, or a decision not to hold one.

## What this cannot claim

The mechanics of seven named techniques the corpus lists without explaining.
