## When

When generation is wanted and verbal sessions are dominated by whoever speaks first. This is
the best-referenced document in the corpus.

## Method

Six participants, three ideas each, five minutes, six rounds, passing sheets. Written and
silent throughout: that is the mechanism, not a detail.

## Output

The full set of written ideas, unfiltered, handed to `converge-on-a-shortlist`.

## What this cannot claim

**That it beats brainstorming.** One source asserts the yield "often exceeds" brainstorming;
the comparison papers it cites are not in the corpus and their findings are not reported. The
corpus's verdict: the claims are *"experiential, not measured"*, and *"more ideas is not the
same as better ideas."* The 1968/1969 publication date is inconsistent within the source
itself and unresolvable from here.
