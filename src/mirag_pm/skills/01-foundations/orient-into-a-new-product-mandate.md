## When

The first question, before discovery or planning. It is also the right response to "what does
a product manager do", which the corpus answers with a scope rather than a task list.

## Method

1. State the discipline's scope and levels from `product-management.md`.
2. Take the three pillars of the role from `product-manager-role.md`.
3. Hand the ownership questions to `negotiate-activity-ownership` rather than answering them
   here.
4. Correct two framings the corpus reports as *"commonly misinterpreted"*: the PM is not the
   "CEO of the product" in any sense involving authority, and the Venn diagram is about
   balancing trade-offs, not managing three domains.

## Output

A mandate: what this role decides, what it influences, what it does not touch.

## What this cannot claim

Anything about authority. The corpus is consistent that the role has none by default.
