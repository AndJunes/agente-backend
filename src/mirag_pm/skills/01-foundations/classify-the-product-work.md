## When

Before applying any method, when the product is not a straightforward customer-facing app —
platform, internal tooling, growth, data. The methods in this corpus are largely written for
one of these and transfer unevenly.

## Method

1. Identify the specialisation from `pm-specializations.md`: four types of product work, each
   with a different customer and a different definition of done.
2. Name what changes: who the user is, what evidence is obtainable, which artifacts are worth
   producing.
3. Flag the methods in this corpus that assume a different type.

## Output

The classification, and the list of practices it invalidates.

## What this cannot claim

A seniority ordering. The depth-before-breadth position in the corpus is marked as one
practitioner's opinion, not a finding.
