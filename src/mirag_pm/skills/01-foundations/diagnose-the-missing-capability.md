## When

A symptom is described and the cause is not obvious: repeated wrong bets, plans that slip,
decisions that get re-litigated.

## Method

1. Map the symptom onto the competency map in `product-manager-skills.md`.
2. Check first whether the problem is product work being done as project work, or the
   reverse. `product-vs-project-management.md` separates the two: one is responsible for a
   product's outcome over its life, the other for delivering a bounded piece of work.
3. Name the competence and what would visibly change if it were present.

## Output

One named gap, with the observable that would confirm it.

## What this cannot claim

That the gap is the person's rather than the organisation's. The corpus has no material on
diagnosing that difference.
