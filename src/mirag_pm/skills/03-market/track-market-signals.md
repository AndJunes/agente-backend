## Method

1. Apply the method the document describes, not the examples it uses.
2. Date every specific claim. `outdated-information.md`: *"Method ages slowly. Findings,
   tools, figures and trends age fast."* and *"Prefer the durable layer — 'how to analyse
   trends' outlives 'these are the trends.'"*
3. Re-verify any tool name or figure before citing it.

## Output

An analysis whose claims carry dates.

## What this cannot claim

That any trend named in the corpus is still current. It was captured up to September 2026.
