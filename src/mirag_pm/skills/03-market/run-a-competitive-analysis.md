## When

After the job is framed. Competitors are defined by the job, not by the industry.

## Method

1. **Use the three-way split**: direct, indirect, and substitute-and-do-nothing. The corpus
   weighs this against a source that folds substitutes into indirect and concludes the
   three-way split *"is the more useful taxonomy"* — because a spreadsheet and Notion are
   what most products actually lose to.
2. Choose comparison criteria **before** collecting data, so the collection is not steered by
   what is easy to find.
3. Collect against those criteria.
4. Feed the substitutes back into `run-a-jtbd-switch-analysis`.

## Output

A competitor map with the do-nothing option treated as a real competitor.

## What this cannot claim

A report structure, a positioning map, Five Forces or a feature matrix — all recorded gaps.
And the corpus warns against parity as a goal: matching features is not a strategy.
