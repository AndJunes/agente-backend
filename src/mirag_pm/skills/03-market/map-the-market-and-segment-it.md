## When

Alongside discovery, not instead of it.

## Method

1. Separate market research from user research. The corpus flags one of its own sources as
   *"a market research article whose five types are largely user-research activities"* and
   supplies its own distinction — use that.
2. Read needs across the three kinds: **functional, emotional, social**. Most requirements
   documents capture only the first.
3. Where a survey is proposed, route through `design-a-survey` and its gate.

## Output

A market description with the three need types separated.

## What this cannot claim

Size, segmentation basis, or how good a secondary source is. All three are recorded gaps, and
sizing in particular is frequently expected of this skill and cannot be delivered.
