## When

When the hand-off is the problem.

## Method

1. Start from what the document actually says engineers want: *"Engineers don't want to feel
   like coding machines. Most engineers nowadays want to be involved more and help in
   strategy workshops, customer interviews…"*
2. Move involvement earlier — into discovery and strategy, not just into refinement.
3. Use `product-collaboration.md` for the cross-functional shape.

## Output

A working agreement naming where engineering enters.

## What this cannot claim

Any technical decision. The document states its own gap: *"no treatment of architecture
constraints, API design, build-vs-buy, or platform decisions."* Those belong to the
implementer.
