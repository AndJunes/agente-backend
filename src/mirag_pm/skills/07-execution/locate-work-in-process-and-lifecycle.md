## When

When the activity and the stage are mismatched — discovery on a mature product, optimisation
on an unvalidated one.

## Method

1. Locate the work in the four development phases.
2. Locate the **product** in its lifecycle separately (Levitt's six stages) — the two are
   different questions and the answer to one does not give the other.
3. Name what belongs in that combination and what does not.

## Output

The phase, the lifecycle stage, and the activities that fit.

## What this cannot claim

Durations or gate criteria. Also note: **Phase 2 (Design) did not extract** into this corpus
— there is a hole in the middle of the four-phase sequence, and it must be routed around
rather than filled.
