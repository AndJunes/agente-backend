## When

A process complaint. These three documents are the corpus's only `primary-text-quoted`
material, so this is one of the few places it can answer from the source.

## Method

1. **Say which text.** The Scrum Guide, the twelve Agile principles and the Kanban Method say
   different things, and a claim attributed to "agile" generally belongs to one of them.
2. Use the four values and twelve principles as a **diagnostic instrument**, not a checklist.
   Principle 7 — *"Working software is the primary measure of progress"* — settles more
   arguments than any ceremony.
3. Where the practice diverges, describe the divergence rather than grading it.

## Output

What the text says, what the team does, and where the two differ.

## What this cannot claim

That fidelity produces better outcomes. No source here measures that. And note what the texts
deliberately omit — Scrum does not define estimation, which `estimate` depends on.
