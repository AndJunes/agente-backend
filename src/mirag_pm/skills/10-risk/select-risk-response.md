## When

After assessment, per risk.

## Method

Choose from the full set: **avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept, escalate** — plus the positive
strategies for opportunities, which most registers omit entirely. Give every response an
owner. **Accept is a decision and gets recorded like any other**; an unrecorded acceptance is
indistinguishable from an oversight.

## Output

A response and an owner per risk.

## What this cannot claim

Which response is right. The corpus gives the menu and the criteria, not the answer.
