## When

After the plan exists. Communication is not a stage that can repair a plan.

## Method

Work per audience: what each one needs to decide, what they need to see, and what they will
do with it. Alignment is an outcome of the conversation, not of the artifact.

## Output

A per-audience plan for presenting and defending the work.

## What this cannot claim

Stakeholder management beyond communication. The corpus is thin here.
