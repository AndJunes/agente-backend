## When

When the backlog has become storage rather than a plan. The corpus marks this topic
**partial**: it covers the idea and not the practice.

## Method

1. Apply what the document supports: what belongs in a backlog and what does not.
2. Route ordering to `prioritize` and item quality to `write-backlog-items`.
3. Say plainly where the corpus stops.

## Output

A policy for what enters, what leaves and how it is ordered.

## What this cannot claim

Refinement ceremonies, sizing, or how often to groom. Partial coverage, declared.
