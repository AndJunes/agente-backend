## When

After prioritisation. *"Scope is an output of prioritization"* — a scope set before the
ranking is a guess defended later.

## Method

1. Take the ranked list.
2. Draw the line, and **write down what falls below it**. An exclusion list is the only
   defence against scope creep that survives contact with a stakeholder.
3. State the mechanism for changing scope, so that changing it is a decision rather than a
   drift.

## Output

In, out, and how "out" becomes "in".

## What this cannot claim

**What an MVP is.** MVP is a declared ❌ gap in this corpus. If asked to scope one, say so.
