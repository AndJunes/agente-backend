## When

Between strategy and go-to-market.

## Method

1. Build the value proposition with one of the four models in the corpus, and say which.
2. Separate **value, price and cost** — `product-value.md` treats them as three things, and
   conflating them is how a value conversation becomes a pricing one the corpus cannot have.
3. Position against the alternatives from `run-a-competitive-analysis`, including doing
   nothing.
4. Where growth model matters, `product-led-growth.md` describes four; treat the claim that a
   CPO's presence indicates product-led growth as the practitioner position it is marked as.

## Output

A value proposition and a positioning statement, each with its model named.

## What this cannot claim

**Pricing** is absent from this corpus. **Product-market fit** is a ⚠ gap — the 40% test is
recorded, but the corpus does not support declaring fit. And there is **no evidence** that
product-led growth outperforms other models.
