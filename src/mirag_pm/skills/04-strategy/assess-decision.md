## When

On a decision, not on a plan.

## Method

Work the six factors from `strategic-thinking.md`, and its separation of what success and
failure would look like. State what evidence would change the answer.

## Output

The decision with its reasoning and its reversal condition.

## What this cannot claim

That product owners do not think strategically. The corpus classifies that claim as
**OPINION**, notes it is stronger than what other sources say, and that it is contradicted by
the measured role ambiguity.
