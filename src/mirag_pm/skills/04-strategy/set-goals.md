## When

After direction exists. Goals before direction are a wish list.

## Method

1. Pick one format and say which: OKRs, NCTs, Dream Maps, MSPOTs, V2MOMs are all in the
   corpus.
2. For OKRs, use `okrs.md` — including its own section on when OKRs do not fit.
3. Hand measurement to `define-metrics-within-known-limits`, which will tell you what this
   corpus cannot support.

## Output

Goals in a named format, with their fit conditions.

## What this cannot claim

**The metric.** Designing a measurement set is a declared ❌ gap. Goals can be stated; how to
measure them cannot be answered from here.
