## When

When the question is whether people can complete a task, not whether they want to.

## Method

1. State the tasks and what counts as completion.
2. Choose moderated/unmoderated and in-person/remote from the corpus's comparison, with its
   trade-offs.
3. Apply the ~5 participants per user group figure **only** to qualitative testing, and say
   it is unexplained in the source.

## Output

A specification, or an audit naming what the test could not have shown.

## What this cannot claim

How to run it, how to do it quantitatively, or anything about accessibility. The document
states its own important limitation, and accessibility is absent from this corpus entirely.
