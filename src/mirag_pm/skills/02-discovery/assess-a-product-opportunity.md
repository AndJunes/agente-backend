## When

Between discovery and strategy, on a specific opportunity.

## Method

1. Work through the corpus's six steps rather than the three-factor version. The corpus is
   explicit that market demand, competition and financial feasibility *"are necessary but not
   sufficient for product work — they omit technical feasibility, strategic fit and risk."*
2. Scale the effort to the bet: ***the depth of validation should scale with the size and
   reversibility of the bet.***
3. Declare what was not assessed.

## Output

An assessment, with its unknowns named as prominently as its findings.

## What this cannot claim

A number. Market sizing is a recorded gap, and the corpus contains no decision threshold that
would turn this assessment into a go or a no-go.
