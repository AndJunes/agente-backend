## When

Before new research. The cheapest evidence is usually already collected and unread.

## Method

1. Gather the existing signals.
2. Separate the need from the requested solution.
3. Cross-check against `run-a-jtbd-switch-analysis` and the market documents.
4. Rank what deserves primary research, and hand that to `scope-a-discovery`.

## Output

Candidate needs, each traced to the signal that raised it.

## What this cannot claim

Two figures in this document are marked **"do not cite"** — that over 90% of unhappy
customers never complain, and the $3.8 trillion cost of poor customer experience. They may be
described as claims the source makes. They may not be repeated as fact.
