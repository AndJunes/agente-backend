## When

**As a gate on every method skill in this domain**, not as a standalone. No interview guide,
survey, observation or usability test is designed until this has run.

## Method

1. Classify the question on the two axes the corpus uses: qualitative/quantitative ×
   behavioural/attitudinal.
2. Read off the methods that can answer a question of that kind, and — more usefully — those
   that cannot.
3. Say plainly when the requested method is the wrong instrument. This is the single most
   common correction the domain supports.

## Output

A method, with the axis reasoning shown.

## What this cannot claim

Sample sizes that transfer. The corpus gives 8–10 interviews and ~5 participants per group
for qualitative usability testing, and states that **these numbers must not be moved between
methods** and are not a general rule. Both figures are additionally flagged as unjustified.
