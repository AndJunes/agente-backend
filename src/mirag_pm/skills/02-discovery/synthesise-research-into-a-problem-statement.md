## When

At the end of discovery, as the hand-off into strategy and planning.

## Method

1. Separate what was observed from what was inferred.
2. Attach each claim to the method that produced it and that method's scope.
3. State what remains unknown — this becomes `openQuestions` downstream, and leaving it empty
   is itself a claim.

## Output

One problem statement, its evidence, and its open questions.

## What this cannot claim

The size of the problem. Quantifying a problem is a recorded capability gap, and there is no
go/no-go threshold anywhere in this corpus.
