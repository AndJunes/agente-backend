## When

After `choose-a-research-method` has confirmed interviews answer this question.

## Method

The seven steps are in the corpus verbatim:

1. Write your research questions
2. Brainstorm interview questions
3. *(map questions to research questions)*
4. Fill in for unaccounted research questions
5-6. *(sequence and phrase)*
7. **Pilot your guide**

Throughout: ask about past behaviour, not future intent. The corpus is repeatedly explicit
that what people say they want and what they do are different things.

## Output

A guide whose every question traces to a research question, piloted before use.

## What this cannot claim

That the interview will be conducted well. The corpus covers the guide, not the moderation.
