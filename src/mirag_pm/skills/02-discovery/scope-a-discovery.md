## When

Before any research method is chosen. Choosing a method first is how discovery becomes
activity rather than evidence.

## Method

1. **Settle which sense of discovery is meant.** The corpus keeps two and says they are not
   interchangeable: a bounded phase converging on a problem statement, and a continuous
   practice of identifying and validating opportunities. Ask which.
2. State the decision the work must inform. Discovery that informs no decision has no
   stopping condition.
3. Apply the principle that recurs across this whole domain, from
   `opportunity-assessment.md`: ***the depth of validation should scale with the size and
   reversibility of the bet.***
4. Hand the assumptions to `surface-and-rank-assumptions` and the methods to
   `choose-a-research-method`. Ideation comes last, not first.

## Output

A scope: the decision, the questions, the stopping condition, and what is out.

## What this cannot claim

How much is enough, in numbers. The corpus is systematically strong on *when not to* and
silent on *how much*: it offers almost no justified thresholds anywhere.
