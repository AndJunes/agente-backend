## When

After the discovery is scoped, never before.

## Method

1. Read the framework catalogue: dual-track, Design Sprint, JTBD, Double Diamond, Lean
   Startup, the Opportunity Solution Tree.
2. Match it to the scope, not to fashion. Each one presupposes a different amount of prior
   knowledge.
3. Attributions — Design Sprint to Knapp/GV, Lean Startup to Ries, the Opportunity Solution
   Tree to Torres, dual-track to Sy — are all marked UNVERIFIED in this corpus. Name them as
   commonly attributed, not as established history.

## Output

A framework with the reason it fits, or an explicit "none of these".

## What this cannot claim

Comparative effectiveness. Nothing here is measured against anything else.
