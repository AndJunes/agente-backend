## When

When the question is about motivation rather than features, and when competitors include
"do nothing" and a spreadsheet.

## Method

1. State the job: the progress someone is trying to make.
2. Identify what is being hired today and what it would be fired from.
3. Feed the result to `run-a-competitive-analysis`, whose substitute-and-do-nothing category
   this directly supplies.

## Output

A job statement and the switching context.

## What this cannot claim

**That JTBD is a single method.** The corpus records Christensen's "jobs" and Ulwick's
outcome-driven innovation as different schools, and attributes the Job Story to the
Klement/Adams/Intercom lineage rather than Christensen. It records this as a gap. The
illustrative cases are UNVERIFIED.
