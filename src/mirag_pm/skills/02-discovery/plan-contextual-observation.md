## When

When the question is about what people do in context and interviews would only capture what
they say.

## Method

1. Confirm via `choose-a-research-method` that the question is behavioural.
2. Plan the observation against that question.
3. **State the generalisability bound in the output itself.** The corpus records a source
   arguing that non-generalisability is a category error, and pushes back: *"a defensible
   methodological position, but it should not be used to wave away the limitation."*

## Output

An observation plan, and findings that carry their own scope.

## What this cannot claim

That observation produces better returns. The claims connecting empathy or ethnography to
financial performance are marked UNVERIFIED, as are the illustrative anecdotes.
