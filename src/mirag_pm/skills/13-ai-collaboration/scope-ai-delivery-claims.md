## When

Any productivity claim. `evidence-on-ai-assisted-delivery.md` is marked **READ FIRST** in its
own domain.

## Method

1. Lead with what the document leads with: *"there is very little rigorous evidence, and it
   does not say what most people say it says."*
2. Give the measurement and its conditions. METR's RCT found experienced developers on mature
   codebases were **slower** while believing they were faster — and the document reproduces
   what the study *explicitly says it does not show*.
3. Name the perception gap as the transferable finding, not the headline number.
4. Where the authors qualified their own result, say so.

## Output

The claim with its scope conditions attached, or a refusal.

## What this cannot claim

That the finding generalises. It is bounded by its study conditions, and the corpus is
emphatic that this is the point rather than a caveat.
