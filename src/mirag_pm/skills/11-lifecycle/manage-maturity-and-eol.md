## When

Maturity or decline. Both are stages, not failures — and the corpus notes the lifecycle loops
back from saturation rather than ending there.

## Method

1. Diagnose against the maturity failure modes: the **feature factory**, the **backlog
   beast**, and the case for **self-disruption**.
2. For decline, work `decline-and-end-of-life.md` — while knowing its limits: the decline
   signal list, the transition options, one source's steps 4–10 and another's four strategies
   **all extracted only partially**. Three of its five steps have holes.
3. Name the options and their consequences; do not pick.

## Output

A diagnosis, the options, what each costs.

## What this cannot claim

When to sunset. No threshold and no timeline exist here, and the document is visibly
incomplete — say so.
