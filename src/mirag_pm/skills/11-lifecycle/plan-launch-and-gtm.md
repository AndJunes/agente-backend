## When

After positioning exists. A launch without it is an announcement.

## Method

1. Take positioning and value from `articulate-value-and-positioning`.
2. Build the GTM: segment, channel, message, and who owns each.
3. Build the launch: what happens, in what order, and what would stop it.
4. Run `build-risk-register` over the launch specifically — launch risk is where the
   product-risk taxonomy earns its keep.

## Output

A launch plan and a GTM approach, with owners.

## What this cannot claim

Pricing or channel economics. Neither is in this corpus.
